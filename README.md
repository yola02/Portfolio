## Projects

### Safest Countries 2026: A Data Visualization Study
Four interactive charts exploring how "safety" is measured differently across the 20 safest countries in the world — comparing a categorical risk rating against multiple numeric safety indices, and examining where those measures agree or diverge.

- **Tools:** Python, Altair (Vega-Lite), pandas
- **Dataset:** [Safest Countries in the World 2026 (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/danishjmeo/safest-countries-in-the-world-2026-data)
- **View it live:** [charts.html](https://yola02.github.io/Portfolio/charts.html)

### Government Debt: Redesigning a Misleading Chart
A before and after study of a Sankey infographic on government debt. The original links two unrelated rankings, so many countries fade out with no match. The redesign is an interactive scatter plot of the same 47 countries, showing debt in dollars and debt as a share of GDP together.

- **Tools:** Vega-Lite, Python, pandas
- **Dataset:** [IMF World Economic Outlook, April 2026](https://data.imf.org/en/datasets/IMF.RES:WEO)
- **View it live:** [government-debt.html](https://yola02.github.io/Portfolio/government-debt.html)

### Same Data, Opposite Conclusions: Global Temperature
Two line charts built from the same yearly global temperature data (2000-2025), one honest and one intentionally misleading. Only the y-axis changes: a zero-based axis with a trend line shows a clear rise, while a very wide axis makes the same data look like warming has stalled. The page explains how each chart was made and how readers can avoid being misled.

- **Tools:** Vega-Lite, Python, pandas
- **Dataset:** [Global Climate & Energy Transition 2000-2026 (Kaggle)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sergionefedov/global-climate-and-energy-transition-2000-2026)
- **View it live:** [climate-temperature.html](https://yola02.github.io/Portfolio/climate-temperature.html)
